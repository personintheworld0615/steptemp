About Us

STEP For A Better World is an NJ-based, student-led nonprofit community organization established in 2013 and inaugurated as a LEO Club in 2019. Sponsored by the West Windsor Lions Club and affiliated with Lions Club International, a global humanitarian organization, the organization is dedicated to serving the underprivileged through service projects, collection drives, and fundraisers while empowering youth to serve selflessly. Our members range from elementary school students to those with advanced degrees across NJ, and our service has spanned 18 countries globally (including United States, India, Nepal, Haiti, The Gambia, Kenya, Uganda, and others).

Our Mission

STEP For A Better World LEO Club is committed to empowering youth and transforming communities through compassionate, strategic service.

  • ✔ Cultivate a generation of socially conscious leaders.
  • ✔ Develop critical life skills via structured volunteering.
  • ✔ Bridge community gaps by forming meaningful partnerships.

Our Vision

We strive to be at the forefront of innovative service projects, from large-scale meal packaging events to record-breaking collection drives.

  • ✔ Lead impactful community initiatives that expand our global footprint.
  • ✔ Foster continuous learning, leadership, and hands-on engagement.
  • ✔ Inspire volunteers to see challenges as valuable opportunities.

Safety

We ensure that all safety precautions are met and promote a safe, fun, inclusive environment.

Training

We train our members to be their best selves and the leaders of tomorrow by developing leadership skills and mentorship.

Education

We encourage volunteers and our communities to become lifelong learners and active participants in positive change.

Partnership

We partner with diverse local and global organizations to amplify our community service impact and expand resources.

Featured Events

Meal Packaging Event

Since May 18, 2021, we have packed 789,722 meals in partnership with OneProject and U.S. Hunger for food pantries across Mercer County.

Guinness World Record

On May 1, 2022, STEP assisted in breaking the Guinness World Record utilizing 100,000 pairs of Spectacles to create the longest chain of Spectacles: 13.030m (42,749 ft and 4.14 inches).

2023 Back to School Drive

In 2023, STEP raised funds to purchase and disperse 202 backpacks filled with 20 different items!

Toy Drive

In 2022, STEP worked with the Toys for Tots Foundation to collect 1,183 toys for children in affiliation with the U.S. Marines Corps Reserve!

Tribute to Heroes

In 2020, STEP raised money to support Princeton Hospital's janitorial staff and local Chinese restaurants affected by xenophobia, recognizing unnoticed local heroes.

2023 Book Drive

In 2023, STEP raised 4,323 books in 19 days to donate to Title One students in Trenton, NJ, to irrigate book deserts.

NJ Governor Awards · 2025

In 2025, STEP was honored with the New Jersey Governor's Volunteer Award in recognition of outstanding service and lasting community impact.

Special Needs Program

Our dedicated volunteers designed and hosted inclusive, engaging programs tailored for children with special needs in the community.

UN Summit of the Future · 2025

In 2025, STEP youth leaders joined the United Nations Summit of the Future, bringing a next-generation voice to global conversations on humanitarian service and community leadership.
