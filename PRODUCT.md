# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Youth volunteers (elementary to college students) seeking local community service opportunities, and their parents/guardians looking for structured, safe leadership programs.

## Product Purpose
To serve as the primary landing page for the STEP For A Better World LEO Club, showcasing local and global humanitarian impact (meals packed, spectacles recycled, book drives) and inspiring youth to join the club.

## Brand Personality
Welcoming, helpful, and dedicated. Warm, inspiring, energetic, yet organized.

## Anti-references
- **Corporate**: Stiff, clinical layouts, dry grids, or sterile colors that feel like a financial firm.
- **Chaotic**: Unstructured columns, hard-to-read font weights, over-saturated color mismatches, or nested grids that create cognitive load.

## Design Principles
- **Warm & Welcoming Atmosphere**: Ensure the typography and color accents evoke friendliness and open-minded community spirit.
- **Visible Human Impact**: Display achievements and event carousels in clean, readable formats that put the community results first.
- **Credible but Youth-Friendly**: Balance vibrant community energy with structured hierarchies so parents and partners trust the club's leadership.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Keep text contrast ratios ≥ 4.5:1 for body and placeholder copy.
- Support prefers-reduced-motion for all counting animations and slide transitions.
